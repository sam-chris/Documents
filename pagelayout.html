Welcome to the Beach

The beach is a wonderful place to relax and enjoy nature. The sound of the waves can help people feel calm and happy. Many people visit the beach to swim, walk, and spend time with their friends and family.

Beaches are also home to many animals and plants. It is important to keep the beach clean by picking up trash and protecting ocean life. Everyone can help keep our beaches beautiful for future generations.
